Paystack, Flutterwave or PayPal: which should your business use?

By VibrantRevolve · 2026-09-24 · 1 min read

Paystack and Flutterwave are built for African payments. They handle cards, bank transfers and other local methods, which is what most Nigerian customers expect to use.

PayPal is familiar to international buyers, so it helps if your customers live abroad. Availability and fees differ by country, so check what your own account can do before relying on it.

Fees, payout speed and supported currencies all change over time. Compare the current terms on each provider's site before you decide.

Offering two options is often better than one: a local gateway for local buyers and PayPal for overseas clients. Fewer abandoned checkouts usually follows.

Our take: We wire up whichever combination fits your customers.
