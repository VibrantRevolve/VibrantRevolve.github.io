Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

VibrantRevolve ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how it's used.

Information We Collect

  • Contact form submissions: name, email address, project type, and project details you provide.
  • Basic technical data collected automatically by standard web analytics (e.g. browser type, general location, pages visited), if analytics tools are enabled.

How We Use It

  • To respond to inquiries submitted through the contact form.
  • To understand and improve how visitors use the site.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

Contact form submissions are processed through EmailJS, a third-party email delivery service. Their handling of data is subject to EmailJS's own privacy policy.

Data Retention

Messages submitted through the contact form are retained only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, unless you request deletion sooner.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data you've submitted by emailing vr@vibrantrevolve.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to vr@vibrantrevolve.com.
