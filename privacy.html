Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

VibrantRevolve ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how it's used.

Information We Collect

  • Contact and booking requests: name, email address or WhatsApp number, project type, preferred call time and the details you provide.
  • Tool follow-up requests: if you choose to leave an email or WhatsApp number after using a free tool, we receive that contact detail and the name of the tool.
  • AI features: text you type into the assistant or AI-powered tools is sent to our AI service to generate a reply.
  • Basic, cookieless site statistics (pages visited, browser type, approximate country) if analytics is enabled. We do not run advertising trackers.
  • Most free tools work inside your browser. Files, logos and invoice details you enter are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us unless a tool says so.

How We Use It

  • To respond to enquiries, booking requests and follow-up requests you send us.
  • To understand and improve how visitors use the site and tools.
  • To deliver AI replies you request.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Legal Basis

We handle your personal data on the basis of your consent (for example when you tick the box on a follow-up request) and our legitimate interest in running and improving the site, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us.

Third-Party Services

We use the following providers, each subject to its own privacy policy: EmailJS (delivering form messages to our inbox), Cloudflare (hosting protection, AI request handling and site statistics), Google Fonts (typefaces), WhatsApp and Telegram (if you choose to message us there), and, when payments are enabled, Paystack, Flutterwave or PayPal (processing payments; we never see or store your card details). Some of these providers process data outside Nigeria.

Data Retention

Messages and follow-up requests are retained only as long as needed to respond to you and keep a record of the work, unless you request deletion sooner.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data you've submitted, object to its use, or withdraw consent, by emailing vr@vibrantrevolve.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to vr@vibrantrevolve.com.
